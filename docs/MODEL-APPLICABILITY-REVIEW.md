# Model applicability review — Haiku, Sonnet 5.5, Opus 5.5, Fable 5.2

Date: 2026-11-12. Scope: all 69 skills in `skills/`.

Method: three Sonnet reviewers each read a third of the `SKILL.md` files in full but only skimmed `references/` and templates. Each classified a skill's content as:

- **A** — project or environment facts a model cannot guess (script names, paths, formats).
- **B** — discipline guardrails against known agent failure modes.
- **C** — generic engineering best practice a frontier model already applies unprompted.

The percentages are estimates, not measurements. Verdicts are judgment calls and not backed by eval runs; rerun `scripts/eval-router.py` and pressure scenarios before acting on any of them.

## Overall verdict

Batch 1 estimates about 60% of its roughly 12.4k words are class C or redundant for Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2. The other batches point the same way, so roughly half the library is dead weight for those two models. What still pays off for them is (1) repo or environment facts and (2) guardrails for long or unattended runs (ask before acting, "skipped is not passed", halt conditions, reproduce a finding before reporting it).

## Overkill for Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2

**Yes — mostly textbook (drop, trim to about 100–250 words, or make opt-in):** `architecture-best-practices`, `choosing-tools-and-substrates`, `designing-layered-pipelines`, `designing-data-pipelines`, `defending-architecture-decisions`, `building-small-cli-tools`, `api-contract-audit`, `dependency-audit`, `code-architecture-review`, `designing-testable-seams`, `evolving-schemas-and-contracts`, `designing-plugin-contracts`, `engineering-for-determinism`, `keeping-event-loops-responsive`, `security-best-practices` (pillars 1–6; keep pillar 7 and `references/private-state-files.md`), `pr-review`, `refactor-execute`, `parsing-untrusted-text-robustly`, `token-finops`.

**Partly — keep a core, move the rest to `references/`:** `compacting-context-safely`, `building-unattended-agent-loops` (keep items 3, 4, 5 and the mistakes list), `building-trustworthy-benchmarks` (keep the gameability test), `clustering-failures-by-root-cause` (keep the ratchet), `designing-exhaustive-test-cases`, `building-code-generators` (keep rules 1, 6, 7), `layered-verification-gates`, `handing-off-sessions`, `release`, `test-coverage`, `using-swe-skills`, `planning-with-contracts`, `writing-user-guides`, `adaptive-model-routing`, `showcasing-graphics-in-docs` + `rendering-graphic-examples` (merge, make opt-in).

## Essential for all four models

- **Non-obvious specifics:** `controlling-processes-safely` (flock-on-inode), `bounding-untrusted-resource-use` (capped-list cursor trap, three required tests), `building-release-binaries`, `writing-pull-requests` (`Closes #12, #15` closes only #12).
- **Guardrails for unattended runs:** `committing-and-releasing-cleanly`, `supervising-autonomous-sessions`, `orchestrating-subagents`, `right-sizing-process`, `running-multi-lens-audits`, `writing-intent-briefs`, `sizing-limits-from-measurement`.
- **Repo-specific mechanics:** `eng`, `keeping-docs-in-sync`, `running-local-ci`, `using-local-tool-registry`, `scaffolding-new-projects`, `drafting-issue-reports`, `hardening-ci-workflows`, `driving-browsers-for-qa`, `typesafe-ai` (opt-in only).

## Per-model notes

- **Opus 5.5 / Fable 5.2:** generic knowledge is wasted context; guardrails still help in long runs. Add a "skip for clear tasks" line to `writing-intent-briefs` and a "skip when reversible" line to `defending-architecture-decisions` so they do not over-ask or over-process.
- **Sonnet 5.5:** gains most from the middle group (checklists and procedures that are cheap to follow); little from the textbook skills.
- **Haiku:** follows short, linear, command-driven checklists well (`running-local-ci`, `scaffolding-new-projects`, `writing-pull-requests`, `committing-and-releasing-cleanly`, the supervising halt list). At risk on branching or long skills: `handing-off-sessions`, `layered-verification-gates`, `github-issues-script`, `designing-plugin-contracts`, `code-architecture-review`, `compacting-context-safely`, `building-unattended-agent-loops`, `building-trustworthy-benchmarks`. It should not build unattended agent loops.

## Cross-cutting problems

1. **"Agent Detection & Mode Tuning" tables and Mermaid flowcharts** in `api-contract-audit`, `dependency-audit`, `code-architecture-review` (plus `AGENT_TUNING.md`), `pr-review`, `refactor-execute`, `test-coverage`: almost no information for Claude Code, and they cost tokens for every model.
2. **Overlap:** `github-issues-script` into `drafting-issue-reports`; `pr-review` / `code-architecture-review` / `running-multi-lens-audits`; `release` / `committing-and-releasing-cleanly`; `showcasing-graphics-in-docs` / `rendering-graphic-examples`; `orchestrating-subagents` / `adaptive-model-routing`; `hardening-ci-workflows` / `running-local-ci`.
3. **Stale or non-Claude content:** `token-finops` rate table lacks Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 and mandates a status line every turn; `adaptive-model-routing` uses Antigravity tier names; `compacting-context-safely` cache-TTL arithmetic may be obsolete.
4. **Always-loaded cost:** `using-swe-skills` has a roughly 70-row routing table (about 700 words) loaded every session.
5. **Cross-reference chatter:** parenthesised skill names in nearly every bullet cost context and invite Haiku to chase other skills.

Overlaps with Phase 4 of the public-readiness plan (de-overlap, move content from 700+ word skills to `references/`).
